Third-party components bundled with Audiobus Remote
===================================================

The project's own code is MIT-licensed (see LICENSE). It bundles these
third-party components, each under its own license:

- SumatraPDF (GPLv3) — invoked as a separate executable (mere aggregation),
  not linked. Source and license: https://github.com/sumatrapdfreader/sumatrapdf
  See also agent/SumatraPDF-LICENSE.txt.

- Cisco OpenH264 (BSD-2-Clause) — bundled as openh264.dll.
  https://github.com/cisco/openh264
